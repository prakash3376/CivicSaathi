CivicSaathi explains Indian government schemes, services and your rights in simple language, in
English, Hindi and Bengali, with the answer tied to a quote from the official document. You can
ask by voice.

Open the app at its own address (`https://<owner>-<space>.hf.space`), not inside the Hugging Face
page: the microphone does not work inside the embedded frame.

Source code, documentation and the rules the assistant keeps are in the project repository.
